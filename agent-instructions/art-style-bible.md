# Art style bible

Status: **STARTER DRAFT**. Snow has not supplied visual preferences or approved a game-specific art direction. This document records a usable foundation, not an approved brand style.

## Foundation

- Favor simple, readable shapes and a small palette suited to quick production.
- Make players, hazards, objectives, and safe space easy to distinguish on a phone screen.
- Give interactive elements clear silhouettes and contrast. Pair essential color cues with shape, labels, or effects.
- Keep mobile UI text legible, touch targets comfortable, and gameplay visible around the controls.
- Match icon, thumbnail, UI, and effects to the actual game's approved core loop. Avoid misleading gameplay claims.
